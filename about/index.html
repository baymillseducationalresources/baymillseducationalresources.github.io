About Bay Mills Educational Resources

Bay Mills Educational Resources (BMER) is an independent educational publishing brand focused on practical, accessible learning.

What We Publish

Our books help readers develop useful skills, explore new hobbies, tackle hands-on projects, and build confidence through clear, beginner-friendly instruction.

Our subjects include home improvement, traditional crafts, technology, outdoor skills, and professional development.

Our Approach

We believe learning should be approachable and useful. Our instructional guides emphasize understandable explanations, practical examples, and activities readers can apply.

Companion Learning Resources

Selected BMER books include free supporting materials, such as practice files, templates, and other downloadable resources.
